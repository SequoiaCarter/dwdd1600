Who am I?

Hey Guys! It’s me, Mothman! (Not actually, I WISH. I just really like the guy, so I named myself after him. And yes, I know in my heart that he’s real). I like to call myself a “cryptic investigationalist.” All things mysterious have me hooked, and I love diving down rabbit holes and doing my own research.

I grew up in rural Kansas, surrounded by big skies and strange stories. Ever since I was a kid, I’ve been obsessed with the unexplained—every creak in the woods, every weird light in the sky. As I got older, that fascination only grew stronger. I couldn’t just wonder about these things anymore—I had to dig deeper, uncover the stories, and piece together the mysteries myself. I started posting about the strange things I saw online whene I got my forst computer, and that's when I found out theres a whole community of fellow cryptid enthusiests just like me! I tend to do more online research then physical, but it's still so facinating for me.

My first investigation that really put me out there was my deep dive into Mothman sightings, wich got a lot of traction. My other more famous adventures is my exploration of the so-called Vampire Estate, and the Bigfoot paper I got published in Cryptids Weekly last year (still my proudest achievement!).
